SENTIVOICETECHNICAL CO-FOUNDER

Sentivoice

A singing and performance coaching product that analyzes recordings and gives targeted feedback. Its audio mode measures pitch and key accuracy, timing, dynamics, vocal strain and expression. Its video mode adds stage presence, movement, audience connection and microphone technique. The AI coach turns results into practice exercises, with a vocal course for structured learning.

My role: technical co-founder. Product features are described from the live website; the specific systems I implemented are not itemized in the CV.

OWL SCOPE NEWS · 2026 onwardTECHNICAL CO-FOUNDER

OwlScope News

An AI news aggregator built for comparing coverage of the same story. Readers can follow custom topics, see stories grouped across publishers, switch among matched sources, and inspect differences in emphasis, framing and omissions. A free Basic tier makes the comparison workflow accessible.

My work: I co-founded and launched the product, owned its architecture and technical execution, integrated AI / LLM analysis, and shipped the v1.5 free tier. I also led early product strategy and engineering delivery.
